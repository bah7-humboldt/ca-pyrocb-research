What makes a pyroCb day?

Each factor below is shown for every fire day, split by what the fire's plume did. A factor is useful if pyroCb days (orange) sit apart from the rest. The score is from a leave-one-fire-out test: the cut is chosen on six fires and tried on the seventh, so a factor that only works for one fire scores badly. The Factor guide explains each factor in depth, with the research it comes from.

How the factors rank

Balanced accuracy when each fire is held out (0.5 = no better than chance, 1 = perfect). Click a bar to jump to that factor.
