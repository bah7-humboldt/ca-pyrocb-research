Weather patterns behind pyroCb days

Every fire day was matched to one of six 500 hPa regimes (A–F), the large-scale height patterns over the northeast Pacific and western North America used on the companion AI ensemble research viewer. Regimes can be forecast days ahead, so they hint at pyroCb potential before a fire's own power is known.

Regimes and what the plume did

Lift = how much more often the regime shows up on that class of day than on all studied days (1 = as often as usual). q700 = median 700 hPa specific humidity at the fire (ERA5).

Regime against mid-level moisture

Every fire day, by regime (jittered sideways) and 700 hPa moisture. PyroCb days need both: one of regimes A, C, D, and moist mid-levels.

Rules from regime and moisture

"Each fire held out" picks the moisture cut without that fire and tests it on that fire, for each fire in turn.

Useful as an early heads-up, days ahead, before fire power is known. It flags many more days than FP/PFT does on the day.
