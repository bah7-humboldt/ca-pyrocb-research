Sounding guide

How each sounding on this site is made, what the numbers in its box mean, and what the example soundings by plume class show. The Soundings page explains how to read the chart itself.

A single day's sounding

  • Where: the HRRR model's hourly analysis (its 3 km grid, the 5 × 5 cells around the point) at the fire's hot front that day, where the most fire power was detected by satellite. The Fires pages map each day's hot front.
  • When: the hour the fire came closest to its pyroCb threshold, the highest FP/PFT of the afternoon. If there was no fire-power reading, the hour of lowest PFT; if neither exists, 15:00 PDT.
  • How high: from the ground to 100 hPa, about 16 km.
  • HRRR describes the air around the fire, not inside the plume, so it shows what the fire had to work with.

The numbers in the box

NumberWhat it is
PFTPyrocumulonimbus firepower threshold (Tory & Kepert 2021): the least fire power, in GW, that can carry a plume to zfc against the wind and still convect. "No pyroCb solution" means no amount of fire power would.
zfc, ΔθfcThe height above ground where the plume becomes free to convect, and how much warmer than the mixed layer it must be to get there.
UMLThe mean wind through the mixed layer, which bends the plume over.
PCAPEPyroconvective CAPE: the plume parcel's buoyant energy from zfc to where it stops rising (J/kg).
DCAPEDowndraft CAPE: the energy a parcel of mid-level air gains sinking to the ground, kept saturated by evaporating rain or ice (J/kg). It starts at the driest point (lowest equivalent potential temperature) in the lowest 400 hPa. High DCAPE means strong downdrafts and gusty, erratic outflow winds are possible, a hazard under any collapsing pyroCb or pyroCu. Computed with MetPy's downdraft_cape (after Gilmore & Wicker 1998).
qML, ML topMoisture and depth of the mixed layer.
FP/PFT and the restWhat the fire actually did that day: fire power against threshold, peak fire power, the coldest cloud top GOES saw over the fire, and the growth.

The example soundings by plume class

These are not averages. Each fire's figure puts three single days side by side, one of each kind:

  • the fire's first pyroCb day;
  • its biggest-growth pyroCu day;
  • its biggest-growth day with no deep plume cloud.

Each is drawn and chosen exactly like a single day's sounding above. A fire with no day of a kind shows only the others. Every pyroCb day, and more pyroCu and no-plume days, are in the browser on the Soundings page.
